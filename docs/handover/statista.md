Resource name: Statista

Source information last reviewed date: May 2026

Vendor: Statista, Inc.

Best known for: Statista is best known as a platform for discovering over 1,000,000 statistics from more than 22,500 sources on over 80,000 topics. Statista provides downloadable data, market outlooks, and company and brand reports, along with insights into industry trends and developments. These statistics are available as .XLS files but are also visualized and can be downloaded as PNG, JPEG, and PPT files. Statista has also begun packaging these statistics and visualizations into thematic reports, such as Industry & Market Reports or Company & Brand Reports, and Outlook reports, such as the Health Market Outlook and Digital Market Outlook reports. It also produces data-driven company and brand rankings in collaboration with major media outlets such as Forbes, the Financial Times, and TIME to identify industry leaders and benchmark performance.

Content:
Market Insights: Market forecast and expert KPIs for 1000+ markets in 190+ countries & territories. 
Consumer Insights: This product was formerly referred to as the Global Consumer Survey. The Global Survey covers 55+ countries and regions, with a documented methodology and questionnaire. 
Company Insights: Provides in-depth information about numerous companies covered in the Company Insights tool through reports focusing on a company’s key figures and information, such as its financials, employees, competitive landscape, market valuation, and contact details.  
Ecommerce Insights: Detailed information for 39,000+ online stores and marketplaces, including 70+ KPIs per store and revenue analytics and forecasts. 

AI features: Statista has introduced a Research AI search tool. Learn more at statista.com/help/research-ai. When searching in Statista, there is the use of AI to summarize results in the sidebar, and allows the user to "continue in Research AI Chat.”

Statista is an extremely intuitive and user-friendly database for discovering statistics, reports, and market outlooks. You can browse content by category, or search using keywords, with options to filter by geography, industry, publication date, and content type. Some features, such as the Business Plan Export and Publication Finder, are less visible, while add-ons like the Global Consumer Survey are slightly less intuitive but offer built-in guided tours. While Statista provides source details and supplementary notes about the source of each statistic, detailed methodological information is typically not available. Overall, Statista is particularly useful for discovering data and identifying trends over time, but researchers may need to consult the source for additional context and methodology.
