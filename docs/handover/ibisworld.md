Description: Vendor: IBISWorld
Best known for: IBISWorld contains industry reports written by in-house analysts for a wide range of industries and a growing number of geographies (as of May 2026 they cover 42 countries) focused in North America, Europe (including the U.K.), and Australasia. They also cover China and Global. They have U.S. reports at the NAICS-code level (e.g. Drug, Cosmetic & Toiletry Wholesaling) as well as ones that are more granular (e.g. Allergy Medicine Manufacturing OTC). They also have state and provincial-level reports for all of the U.S. and Canada. A platform upgrade in late 2023 added detailed "call prep" questions that will be useful to students preparing for informational or job interviews. ; alternatives: FirstResearch (fairly similar to IBISWorld in terms of US based NAICS-industry report coverage)

* MarketLine (has a wide variety of content, including US-based industry reports)
* Market report providers like [Mintel](https://sites.google.com/view/bisdex/sources-reviews/mintel), [BCC Research](https://sites.google.com/view/bisdex/sources-reviews/bcc-research), MarketResearch.com, and others can often be used in similar ways
* Industry-specific platforms will also have similar content and be more targeted to that industry (e.g. GlobalData Power or SNL Energy)

* Business Source products (includes industry reports from other vendors like MarketLine)
* ProQuest One Business & ABI/INFORM (includes industry reports from other vendors like Fitch Solutions, FirstResearch) 
* CFRA Industry Surveys (available in S&P products like NetAdvantage and [Capital IQ](https://sites.google.com/view/bisdex/sources-reviews/capital-iq)) ; AI features: Introduced a "Paragraph Analysis" switch that toggles between list-style and paragraph analysis. IBISWorld is explicit in that they use Generative AI to adapt their human-generated list-style analysis into paragraph form.
