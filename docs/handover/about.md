About BISdex
BISdex reviews information sources for business information professionals. Think of it as Wirecutter for business research: instead of consumer products, we evaluate databases and datasets. Each review gives you the essentials up front, so you can quickly decide whether a source deserves a closer look.
What we cover

* Established and emerging databases that libraries can subscribe to or purchase (e.g., IBISWorld, PitchBook, ImpactAlpha, Calcbench).
* Sources researchers can obtain on their own, free or for a fee.
* A wide range of content areas, from bread-and-butter financials to labor market data, ESG, and niche industry coverage.

Our history
Work on BISdex began in 2021, and the site launched in fall 2022. BISdex is a project of the Business Information Sources Committee within the Business Reference and Services Section (BRASS) of the Reference and User Services Association (RUSA), a division of the American Library Association (ALA).
