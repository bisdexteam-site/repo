## Purpose and scope

BISdex publishes reviews of commercial business databases written by librarians who use them. This policy explains what we accept, how submissions are moderated, and how we keep reviews fair and trustworthy. It applies to every review submitted through the BISdex review form.

## Core principles

Four commitments guide every editorial decision.

- **Authenticity.** Reviews come from real information professionals and librarians describing their own hands-on use of a resource.
- **Accuracy.** Descriptive information about a resource, including content, features, and access notes, should be correct and verifiable.
- **Integrity.** We do not allow fake reviews, coordinated campaigns, or misleading claims.
- **Objectivity.** Reviews are personal assessments. They reflect each reviewer's context and judgment. Opinions in a review belong to its author, not to BISdex, the Business Information Sources Committee, BRASS, RUSA, or ALA.

## Who may submit and what we expect

Only people who have used a resource themselves may review it. Vendors, their employees, and anyone paid or rewarded by a vendor may not submit reviews.

A review must:

- Describe your own experience with the resource, honestly and in good faith
- Avoid confidential information, such as pricing or terms covered by a nondisclosure clause
- Disclose any relationship with the vendor that a reader would want to know about

We encourage specific examples, such as a research task the resource handled well or poorly. A review will not be rejected only because it is brief or general.

## Use of generative AI

You may use generative AI tools to help organize or polish a review, as long as the experience and opinions are your own.

- Tell us on the submission form if you used an AI tool and how.
- Revise any AI-assisted text so it reflects what you actually observed, in your own words.
- AI-assisted reviews meet the same standards as every other review.

Reviews written mainly by AI, or describing experiences the reviewer did not have, will be rejected.

## How reviews are moderated

Every review is read by a BISdex moderator before it is published. Nothing goes live automatically.

1. **Submission.** A reviewer completes the Electronic Resource Review Form, either from the "Leave a review" button on a source page or for a resource not yet listed.
2. **Eligibility check.** A moderator confirms the submission is complete, concerns a business information resource, and appears to come from a real user with first-hand experience.
3. **Content review.** The moderator checks the review against this policy, looking for false statements of fact, personal attacks, confidential terms, and undisclosed conflicts of interest.
4. **Wording for legal risk.** Where a statement could be read as a factual claim rather than an opinion, the moderator may ask the reviewer to reword it or to frame it as personal experience.
5. **Decision.** The moderator approves the review, returns it to the reviewer for revision, or declines it.
6. **Publication.** Editors post approved reviews to the resource's Reviews section.

Moderators may correct spelling and formatting. They will not change a review or the substance of their assessment.

## Reviewer privacy

Published reviews do not show the reviewer's name or institution. Instead, each review carries a short descriptor based on Carnegie classification and a range of experience, for example "R1 librarian with 3 to 5 years of experience."

Moderators see the reviewer's contact details only so they can follow up. BISdex does not share those details with vendors or the public.

## Fair treatment of reviews

We apply the same standards to every review, whether it praises a resource or criticizes it.

- We never ask for positive reviews only.
- We do not hide, delay, or bury critical reviews.
- Vendors cannot pay for or request removal of specific reviews.
- A review is declined only for failing this policy, never for its tone or conclusion.

## Declined and removed reviews

If a review is declined or later removed, we will tell the reviewer why by email. Reviewers may revise and resubmit.

We may remove a published review that we learn breaks this policy, for example one that turns out to be fake or to contain a false statement of fact.

## Corrections and concerns

Anyone, including a vendor, may report a factual error. Moderators will review the report and respond. Vendors may correct factual errors in "About this resource" content by communicating changes via email.

## Disclaimer

Reviews are the views of their authors. BISdex makes no guarantee that reviews are complete or accurate and is not responsible for statements made in them.
