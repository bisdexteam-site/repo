BCC Research has a clean, intuitive interface. A single search bar covers all reports and also surfaces related blog and podcast content. One quirk: browsing by category (Nanotechnology, for example) only shows BCC Publishing reports, so use search to find partner content. Partner report pages usually list highlights, figures, and tables, but you have to download the PDF to read the report itself. Scholarly use is limited, though a fair number of applied science papers cite its market sizing data when discussing commercial applications. Historical coverage within academic accounts is thin, and BCC’s own reports are the main draw.
